# Sample pages

Six pages from the **test split of IndicDLP** (Nath et al., ICDAR 2025; AI4Bharat), redistributed under the
dataset's MIT licence as stated on https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/indicdlp. They let the app be
tried without uploading a document. They were chosen by a fixed rule (the first test page of the given
language and domain with at least 5 labelled elements), not by how well the models do on them.
